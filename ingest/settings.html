Machines

Each machine reports into the ledger with its own token. Edit the name, model, acquisition and lease, then save the row. Purchased machines have no lease, so the lease fields are cleared and disabled; record the purchase itself under purchases.

A machine id cannot be renamed because its history is write-once and hash-chained. To use a new id, add a machine and remove the old one. Removing a machine revokes its token and hides it here; its history is kept.
